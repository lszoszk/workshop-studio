How was this made, and how do I take it with me?

  1. Records are the paragraphs in the file, counted in the browser: headline total = number of records; breakdown = records per type, spelled as in the data.
  2. Documents = distinct symbol; years = lowest and highest year. Dates are the document's date, not the date a recommendation was made or accepted.
  3. Filters combine with AND. Keyword: every word must appear in the text or the symbol (literal, lower-cased). Year range is inclusive; SDG matches the goal number.
  4. Theme counts: each theme counts once per record, so they add up to more than the number of records. Labels are the UN's; the app never changes them.
  5. Paragraph number = the number at the start of the text (e.g. 71 or 144.43). Recommending State is read only from UPR texts, from the parentheses at the end; a name with a comma or over 60 characters is rejected.
  6. Nothing is estimated, no model is called, and nothing leaves your browser except what you copy.
