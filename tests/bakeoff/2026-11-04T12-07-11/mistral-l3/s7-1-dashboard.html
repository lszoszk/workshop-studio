How each number is computed

The dashboard counts and filters the records in the UHRI file for . The headline total is the number of records in the file. The breakdown per type uses the values exactly as spelled in the data. Documents are distinct symbols; years are the minimum and maximum year of a document. Filters combine with AND: mechanism, body, type, year range, SDG, keyword (literal substring match). The theme chart counts each theme once per record; the top ten are ranked by count, ties alphabetical. The timeline counts records per year per mechanism. Paragraph numbers are extracted from the start of the text; UPR States from the end. Nothing is estimated and no model is called.
