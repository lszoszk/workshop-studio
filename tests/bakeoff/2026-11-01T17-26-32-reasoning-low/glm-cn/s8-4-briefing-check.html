How this is computed: the checker reads the text you provide and compares each recognisable claim against the recorded vote for the selected resolution — the official totals (yes / no / abstain) and the roll call of the 47 Council members. "Cannot be checked in this data" means exactly that: the data here has no answer for the claim. It is not the same as "wrong" — the claim may well be true, but this dataset (one roll call per resolution) simply cannot confirm it. Counting and matching are done by this page's code, deterministically; nothing is estimated.
