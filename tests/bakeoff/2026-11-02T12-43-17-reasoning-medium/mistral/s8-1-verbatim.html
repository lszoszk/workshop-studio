General Comments search

Words are combined with AND unless you write OR; NOT excludes a word; "quotes" find an exact phrase. Example: "best interests" AND child.

How this is computed: the committee and year filters are applied first, then the word search runs on the paragraph text only (case-insensitive, whole words; quoted phrases match the exact sequence). All counting is done in your browser on the loaded records — nothing is estimated.

How this is computed: each matching paragraph is counted once, under its committee, for the current search and filters — the bars add up to the N in the count line above.
