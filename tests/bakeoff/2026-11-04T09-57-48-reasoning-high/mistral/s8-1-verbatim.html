Syntax. A space or AND joins words; OR; NOT or a leading minus; “an exact phrase” in double quotes; parentheses; a star at the end of a word for any ending (child* finds children). Operators in capitals — lower-case “or” is a word.

Whole words, any case. Curly and straight quotes, apostrophes, dashes and hyphens count as the same, and footnote marks are skipped, so State's finds State’s and a phrase can cross a footnote.

    How this is computed

    Normalised text. Each stored paragraph is normalised once at load: footnote marks [[fn:N]] removed; curly quotes and apostrophes → straight; en/em dashes and soft hyphens → “-”; all whitespace (including non-breaking spaces and line breaks) → one space; lower-cased. The query is normalised the same way, so curly quotes pasted from Word still match.

    Query. Tokens: a phrase in double quotes (straight or curly), ( ), AND / OR / NOT in capitals, a leading minus as NOT, any other run of characters as a word with an optional trailing *. Adjacent terms are joined by AND. Precedence NOT > AND > OR; parentheses override. The parser returns a tree and an English echo, or the error shown in the box.

    Matching. A word becomes a regular expression over the normalised text with letter/number boundaries, so “state” also matches “non-State”; a trailing * allows any ending; a phrase is its words joined by whitespace. A record matches when the tree is true. Whole words only: child does not find children — type child*.

    Highlight. The same positive terms become one regular expression over the shown text, where quotes, apostrophes and dashes match their straight and curly forms and a gap between phrase words may contain footnote marks; matches are wrapped in <mark>. NOT terms are never highlighted.

    Citation and copy. Citation = symbol + pinpoint (“para. N”, “preamble”, “subpara. (b)”); when a symbol is shared by several General Comments, the committee and the document’s name come first and the symbol goes in parentheses. Copy uses the clipboard API with a selectable fallback. Nothing is generated: every character shown or copied comes from a record of the file.
