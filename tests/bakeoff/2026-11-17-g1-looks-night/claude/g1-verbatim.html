What this tool cannot do
  • It finds the words you typed, not the meaning. A paragraph on the same subject in other words is not found: no synonyms, no summaries, no translation (English only).
  • It searches only the attached General Comments and General Recommendations. No concluding observations, no case law, no later or newer texts, and it does not know whether a text has been replaced.
  • An unquoted word matches words that start with it: "child" finds "children", but "children" does not find "child". Quote a phrase to match it exactly.
  • Footnote marks such as [[fn:12]] stay in the text as stored; the footnotes they point to are not shown. A phrase broken by a footnote mark still matches.
  • The citation is the symbol and paragraph number as stored. Where one symbol covers several documents, the short title is added. Check it against the original by clicking the symbol.
  • Nothing leaves your browser: no network, no model.
