Briefing checker — Human Rights Council recorded votes

1 · Pick a recorded vote

How this is computed: the “official totals” are the yes / no / abstain fields of the selected record; the “roll call” is counted on this page from the record’s votes map (one entry per Council member). For a few records the two differ because of gaps in the source — both are always shown, never silently picked. The chart plots both.

2 · Briefing text (AI or your own)

The button below sends only the symbol and the title of the selected resolution to the language model — none of the workshop data. It is slow and costs money; you can press Stop and the answer will be discarded. The answer lands in the text box marked data-source="ai"; you can edit it or paste your own text at any time.

Claims the checker recognises in the text: numbers such as “29 votes in favour”, “16 against”, “1 abstention”, “29-16-1”; dates such as “7 July 2026”, “July 7, 2026” or “2026-07-07”; the session such as “51st session”; and State votes such as “Kenya voted in favour”, “France voted against”, “China abstained”, “X did not vote”.

3 · Check the text against the recorded vote

matches the recorded vote agrees with the claim wrong the recorded vote directly contradicts the claim cannot be checked this data has no value to compare against

“Cannot be checked” is not the same as “wrong”. “Wrong” means the recorded vote contradicts the claim (for example the text says 30 in favour but the record says 29). “Cannot be checked” means this dataset simply has nothing to compare — for example the State is not in the roll call for this vote (the votes map has gaps), or the record has no session number. It is an absence of evidence here, not evidence of an error.

How this is computed: every claim above is found by deterministic text matching (regular expressions) in the text box and compared with the selected record’s fields (yes/no/abstain totals, the roll call counted from the votes map, date, session, votes). Counting, filtering and matching are done by this page’s code — nothing is estimated, and no AI is used for the check.
