How the app checks itself

Check the UN's own labels

Draw ten records at random from the current filter and judge, for each, whether its theme labels fit the paragraph. This is your judgement of the UN's labels; nothing is sent anywhere.

Method & export

Every number on this page is a loop over the records in WORKSHOP_DATA.uhri, computed in your browser when you open it; nothing is estimated and no model is called. The headline total is the number of records in the file; the breakdown under it counts records per type, spelled as in the data. "Documents" counts distinct symbols; "years" takes the minimum and maximum of year.

Filters combine with AND: exact match on mechanism, body and type; year between the two selects; an SDG counts when the record's sdgs list contains the goal; a keyword matches when every word occurs as a literal substring of the record's text or symbol. Theme counts count each theme once per record, so the ten bars can add up to more than the record count; percentages are of records, not labels. The paragraph number is the number at the start of the text; the recommending State is read from the parentheses at the end of a UPR text and is counted once per record per State.

Dates are document dates, not the dates recommendations were made or accepted.

Data

Charts follow the UK Government Analysis Function guidance on charts and tables (horizontal ranked bars, values shown, a table under the chart). Citations follow the Dag Hammarskjöld Library's advice: symbol as key, pinpoint kept. Transparency of method follows OHCHR's Human Rights-Based Approach to Data.
