General Comments search

Words are combined with AND unless you write OR; NOT excludes a word; "quotes" find an exact phrase. Plain words match whole words only ("right" does not match "rights"). Example: "best interests" AND child.

How this is computed: the committee and year filters are applied first; the second number counts the paragraphs left after filtering, and the first number counts those whose text matches the search. The chart and the cards use this same list, so the bars add up to the first number.

Matching paragraphs by committee

How this is computed: each bar counts the matching paragraphs (after filters and search) grouped by their committee field. Committees with no matching paragraph are omitted, so the bars always add up to the number of matching paragraphs.
