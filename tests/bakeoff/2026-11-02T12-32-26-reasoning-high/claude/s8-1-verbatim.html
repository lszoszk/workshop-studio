General Comments search

Words are combined with AND unless you write OR; NOT excludes a word; "quotes" find an exact phrase. Write AND, OR, NOT in capitals. Example: "best interests" AND child. Only whole words match ("right" does not find "rights").

Matching paragraphs per committee

How this is computed: your browser first applies the committee and year filters, then the search to the paragraph text. The bars count the paragraphs on that same list, so they add up to the number of matches above. Nothing is estimated.
