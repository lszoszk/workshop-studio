    How each number is computed

    The headline total is the number of records in the file; the breakdown counts records per type, spelled as in the data. Documents counts distinct symbols; years are the minimum and maximum of year. Filters combine with AND: exact match on mechanism, body and type; year inclusive between the two selects; SDG matches a record's list of goals; keyword requires every word to occur in the text or the symbol, literally.

    Theme counts count each theme label on each filtered record once, so bars add to more than the record count; the percentage is of records. The timeline counts records per year and mechanism. The paragraph number is the leading number of the text; the recommending State comes from parentheses at the end of UPR recommendations only.
