      What this tool cannot do

      • It finds only the words you type. No synonyms, no paraphrase, no summaries: "kids" will not find "children".
      • A word also finds longer words that begin with it (child finds children, childhood). It does not find words that change earlier, such as woman and women.
      • It searches only the attached General Comments and General Recommendations. No concluding observations, no case law, no other languages.
      • Footnote marks such as [[fn:12]] are shown as the file stores them and are not searched. The tool cannot check a paragraph against the UN original: use the symbol link to compare.
