What this tool cannot do

  • It matches the words you type, not their meaning: no synonyms, no summaries, no translation. English only.
  • Only the General Comments and General Recommendations in the attached file: no concluding observations, no case law.
  • Case and apostrophe shape are ignored, nothing else. A hyphen or a different spelling is a different word, and a word in quotation marks must match whole.
  • Footnote marks such as [[fn:12]] are kept as the file stores them, and the text of the footnotes is not in the file.
  • A symbol is not always one document. When a symbol holds several titles (for example A/48/18), the title is added to the citation. A joint General Comment carries two symbols in one field, shown as stored.
  • It cannot confirm that the file's wording is the final published wording: open the linked document and compare.
  • Nothing leaves your browser: the page uses no network.
