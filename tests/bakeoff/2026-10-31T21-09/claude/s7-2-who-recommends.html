The 15 States that made the most recommendations

How this is computed: only records whose mechanism is "Universal Periodic Review" and whose type is "Recommendations" are used. The recommending State is the text inside the last parentheses at the very end of the paragraph, e.g. "(Croatia)". If the parentheses hold several States separated by commas or semicolons, each State is credited with the recommendation. Parentheses that contain a number or are very long (such as "(see para. 12)") are not treated as a State. Click a bar to list that State's recommendations.

Which SDGs does each State raise most?

How this is computed: for each of the 15 States, we count how many of its recommendations carry each SDG tag (a recommendation can carry several SDGs, or none) and show the three most frequent. Numbers in brackets are recommendations. Click a row to list that State's recommendations.
