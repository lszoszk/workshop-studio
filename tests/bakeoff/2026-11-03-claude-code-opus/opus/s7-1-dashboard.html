UN human rights recommendations and observations

Every number on this page is counted in your browser from the file. Nothing is estimated, no AI model is called, and nothing leaves the page except what you copy or download.

At a glance

Filters

Literal match, not synonyms: every word must appear in the text or symbol.

How this is computed: all filters combine (AND) and feed the tiles, both charts, the table and the export. Mechanism, body and type are exact matches; years are inclusive.

Themes

Ten most frequent themes

How this is computed: each theme label on a filtered record counts once; a record can carry several themes, so the bars add up to more than the number of records. Percentages are of records, not of labels; click a bar or a theme name to add it as a filter.

Over time

Records per year by mechanism

How this is computed: filtered records counted per year and mechanism; every year from the first to the last is shown, so a year with no records is a visible gap. Years are the document's date, not the date a recommendation was made or accepted.

How the app checks itself

Each line compares two values computed in different ways. A failed check is shown in red with both values; nothing is hidden.

    Chart against table (current filter)

      Random-sample label check

      This is your judgement of the UN's labels; nothing is sent anywhere.

      How this is computed: ten records are drawn from the current filter with a seeded random generator (mulberry32). The same seed and the same filter always give the same ten records, so a colleague can check your sample.

      Export

      How this is computed: the CSV holds the table's columns plus the full text and all labels of every matching record, preceded by lines naming the country, source, build date, filters and row count. The summary is a plain-text paragraph with the headline numbers and the ten themes.
