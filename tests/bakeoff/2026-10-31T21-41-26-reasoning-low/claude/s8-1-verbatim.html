General Comments search

How the search works: words next to each other are treated as AND. Use OR, NOT (or a minus sign before a word), and "quotes" for exact phrases. Matching ignores upper/lower case. Use ( ) for grouping. Every paragraph is tested by code, nothing is estimated.

Hits per committee

How this is computed: each bar counts the paragraphs matching the search and the year range, ignoring the committee filter so you can compare committees. The selected committee is shown in the darker colour.
