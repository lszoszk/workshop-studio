Suggest SDGs with AI (experimental — sends record texts to an external model; never runs on page load)

How this works: takes the first 50 records matching the filters above (same order as the table below, newest first; if fewer match, all of them), sends their full paragraph texts to a language model in batches of 25 via studio.ask(prompt, {json:true}), and asks for JSON mapping each record id to SDG numbers 1–17. The UN's own SDG tags come from the data; the agreement rate is computed locally. Only the “AI SDGs” column comes from the model.

Cells tagged AI (attribute data-source="ai") contain values returned by the model. Everything else on this page is computed locally in your browser.
