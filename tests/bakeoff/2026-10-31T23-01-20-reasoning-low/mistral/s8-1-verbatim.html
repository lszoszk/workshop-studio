Search the General Comments of the UN treaty bodies

Syntax: AND, OR, NOT in capitals; exact phrases in "quotes". Neighbouring terms are joined with AND. Matching is case-insensitive; single words must match a whole word.

How this is computed: your query is parsed in this page (precedence NOT > AND > OR; AND implied between neighbouring terms) and evaluated against the full text of every paragraph that passes the committee and year filters. The bars count matching paragraphs per committee among the current results. All counting is done locally and exactly — nothing is estimated.

About the results: each row shows the paragraph text exactly as stored in the dataset — only the matched search terms are highlighted, nothing is summarised or paraphrased. The symbol links to the UN document (opens in a new tab); “Copy citation” copies e.g. CCPR/C/GC/37, para. 12 to the clipboard.
