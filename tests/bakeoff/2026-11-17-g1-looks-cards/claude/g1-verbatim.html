      What this tool cannot do
      • It does not summarise, paraphrase or look for synonyms: it finds only the words you typed. "Disabled" will not find "disability".
      • It searches only the attached General Comments file: no concluding observations, no case law, no other sources.
      • It is English only and does not translate.
      • It matches letters, not meaning: a dash or hyphen written differently from yours ("non-discrimination" or "non discrimination") is a different word. Apostrophe shape and capital letters are ignored.
      • Footnote marks such as [[fn:12]] are shown as stored but are not searched, so a phrase interrupted by a footnote mark is not found as one phrase.
      • The file may not hold every paragraph of every document. Before you quote, open the symbol link and compare the paragraph with the original.
      • "Copy citation" gives the symbol and paragraph number only. For a symbol that holds several documents or titles, it adds the short title.
