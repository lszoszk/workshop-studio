Spot-check the SDG tags

Draw 10 random paragraphs from the current selection, read each one, and mark whether the SDGs the UN index attached to it are right, wrong or arguable.

How this is computed: your browser picks the records at random (each matching record has the same chance) from the records that pass the filters at the moment you click. The SDGs shown are those stored in the data for each paragraph (the UN index); no AI is involved, and the marks are only yours. Right = the SDGs fit the paragraph. Wrong = they do not fit, or an obviously relevant SDG is missing. Arguable = a reasonable person could disagree. The agreement rate is the number marked right divided by the number checked; arguable counts as not agreeing. With only 10 records it is a rough indication, not a precise measure of the whole index.
