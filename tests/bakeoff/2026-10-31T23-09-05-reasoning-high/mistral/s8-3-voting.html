Timeline by year

How this is computed: for each year, the bars count the recorded votes in the selected scope in which both selected States appear in the roll call, split into “same vote” vs “different vote” (identical Y/N/A code or not). The dark line shows the share voting the same that year, on the right-hand axis.

Votes where they differed

How this is computed: every recorded vote in the selected scope where both States took part but their vote codes differ, most recent first. The symbol links to the UN document (or the OHCHR vote record when no document link exists). Rows marked ⚠ totals ≠ roll call are cases where the official totals (yes/no/abstain) do not match the roll call counted from the record’s votes map — both figures are shown in the tooltip.
