How this is computed

Load. One pass over all records. Per record: the normalised text, the shown text (footnote marks removed), the mark count, the leading number, the pinpoint, the citation, and the committees found in the symbol plus the committee field.

Normalisation. [[fn:N]] marks are skipped; curly and straight quotes and apostrophes, en/em dashes, hyphens and non-breaking spaces are folded to one form; everything is lower-cased. The query is normalised the same way, so curly quotes pasted from Word still match.

Query. Tokens: a phrase in double quotes (straight or curly), parentheses, AND / OR / NOT in capitals, a leading minus as NOT, any other run of characters as a word with an optional trailing star. Adjacent terms are joined by AND; precedence NOT, then AND, then OR; parentheses override. A word becomes a regular expression over the normalised text with letter/number boundaries, so “state” also matches “non-State”; a star allows any ending (child* finds children).

Matching. The Boolean tree is evaluated over the normalised text of every record; the committee, year and document filters are applied after. Default order: year descending, then title, then paragraph; the select swaps the comparator, the hit set does not change.

Citation and quotation. Assembled only from the record’s own fields — symbol, paragraph, title, committee, doc_url — never invented. Preambles are cited as “preamble”, lettered sub-paragraphs as “subpara. (b)”, shared symbols carry the committee and the document’s name first. When the text begins with its own number and that number equals the paragraph field, the copied quotation drops the leading “12. ” because the citation carries it; the shown text keeps it.

Honesty. This page retrieves; it never generates. No model is called and nothing leaves the browser. Counting, filtering, sorting and matching are done by this page’s code, deterministically.
