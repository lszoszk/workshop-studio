Comparing two States' votes at the Human Rights Council

Summary

    How this is computed: each recorded vote (roll call) in the dataset is included only if both selected States cast a vote (Yes, No or Abstain) in it, and it matches the vote-type filter. "Voted the same way" counts votes where both States cast an identical vote. Nothing is estimated.

    Agreement over time

    How this is computed: for each year, the share of joint votes where the two States voted identically, and the number of joint votes. Years with no joint votes are not shown.
