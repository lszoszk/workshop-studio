Build the workshop's apps without the studio

This page is only the template of the kit, with no data inside. Download your kit from the studio: About in its footer → Download the take-home kit.

  1. Copy a request below and paste it into a chatbot: your own (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot), or LM Arena (free, no account: Direct chat, then pick a model).
  2. When the answer is complete, copy all of it (the chatbot's copy button) and paste it here, under 2. The app runs on the right, on the workshop data in this file.
  3. Save as one file keeps the app with its data: it opens with a double-click, also with no internet.

Your own chatbot at work is the best place: you keep using it after the workshop, the conversations stay in your account, and the studio's budget is left to others. Some personal plans train on your conversations unless you switch it off. On LM Arena, what you type may be shared for research: paste only these requests and public material.
