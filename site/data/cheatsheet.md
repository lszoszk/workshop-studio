# Five paragraphs for a prompt

Ethan Mollick's questions for delegating work to an AI, as the five paragraphs of the prompt card. Read his two essays: [Management as AI superpower](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/management-as-ai-superpower) and [Agency and agents](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/agency-and-agents).

- **Goal and why.** What are we trying to accomplish, and why? Say who the tool is for and what it is for, in your own words.
- **Before you build.** What interim outputs do you need to follow progress? Ask the model to say what it sees in the file and to write a plan first.
- **Limits.** Where are the limits of the delegated authority? Name the data it may use, what it must never change, and what must stay on your computer.
- **Done looks like.** What does done look like? Describe what you will type, what you will see, and what you can copy.
- **Check before you finish.** What should the model check before it tells you it is finished? Ask it to show its counts and to say what the tool cannot do.

# Habits that help

- **Plan first.** Ask for a plan and read it before anything is built. Changing a plan is cheaper than changing an app.
- **Give the data, not a PDF.** A clean file lets the code count and quote exactly. A chatbot reading a document paraphrases it.
- **Test before you trust.** Search for a phrase you know and compare the result with the source. Open the link and read the original paragraph.
- **Ask for one change at a time.** Then check it. Several changes at once make it hard to see which one broke something.
