How this is computed: rows are the filtered records, sorted by date, then body, then paragraph number (numeric), then file order. Click a row to read the full paragraph. State = names in the last parentheses of a UPR text; "State not found" when they do not look like States.

How was this made, and how do I take it with me?

Every number on this page is a count made by a loop over the records of the attached file, in your browser. Nothing is estimated, no model is called and nothing leaves the page except what you copy.

Filters combine with AND. Mechanism, body, type and State match exactly; the year range is inclusive; SDG matches the goal number; every word of the keyword must occur literally in the text or in the document symbol; each theme chosen from the chart must be carried by the record.

Themes: each label counts once per record, so theme counts add up to more than the number of records, and percentages are shares of records. A label that only appears in old years may be a legacy label: read its year range and its sample in FIG.01 before you rely on it.

The paragraph number is the number at the start of the text (for example 71 or 144.43); texts that do not start with one have none. The recommending State is read only for Universal Periodic Review records from the last parentheses of the text; if they contain a comma or are longer than 60 characters, the record shows "State not found".

Dates are the dates of the documents, not of the recommendations. This dashboard does not say whether a UPR recommendation was accepted, because the file does not contain it.

The closed heading "Every check" on the Overview tab compares the app's own numbers with each other and lists what does not match.
