The plan this app was built from · Mistral Large 4

Goal

A research wiki for diplomats and NGO, NHRI and UN staff: what nine documents say about one concept in neurotechnology and justice, every quote opening its original page.

The data

WORKSHOP_DATA.library, one record per document: id, symbol, kind, author, year, short_title, url; from each of its passages: id, page, page_label, paragraph, section, text.

What the app shows

  1. Sidebar: the concept pages with passage counts: mental privacy, freedom of thought, self-incrimination, lie detection, consent, neural data, data solidarity, proportionality. Below, "Add a concept": type a term, press Enter.
  2. Page heading: the concept and the terms that find it (lie detection also "deception detection", "brain fingerprinting").
  3. Table "Passages by kind of document": one row per kind, and a total.
  4. The passages, grouped by document, in library order. Group heading: short_title, kind, year, symbol linked to url. Each passage: its citation as a link calling studio.openSource(id, page), the section if any, the text folded after three lines.
  5. "Related concepts": other concepts found in the same passages, as links.
  6. Button "Draft a summary": sends at most the first 12 passages on the page with await studio.ask(prompt); the answer appears in a box labelled "Written by AI. N passages were sent to the model and left the browser".

Rules for the code

  • A passage uses a concept when its text contains one of the terms, ignoring case and line breaks. Table, groups, sidebar and related concepts use the same list.
  • text is shown character for character; the fold hides, never shortens.
  • Citation: symbol + ", para. " + paragraph when paragraph is set; otherwise author, year and printed page, e.g. "Szoszkiewicz & Yuste 2025, p. 3471". Journal articles are cited by surnames ("Szoszkiewicz & Yuste"; "Prainsack et al." for three or more), other documents by author as written. openSource gets page, never page_label.
  • The prompt lists the passages with their citations and asks for three sentences, each ending with the citation it rests on; the model may rest on only the passages it was sent. The answer sits inside data-source="ai"; never called on page load.
  • No symbol → show author; no section → omitted; no passages → "No passage uses this term", no button.

How we will know it works

  1. Click "mental privacy": the table rows add up to its total; the Journal article row is not zero.
  2. Click "A/81/351, para. N": the PDF opens where that paragraph starts.
  3. Open "lie detection": one passage contains "deception detection" but not "lie detection".
  4. Click "Draft a summary": three sentences, each ending with a citation also on the page; the label says how many were sent.

Not in this version

  • No adding terms to an existing concept; added concepts are lost on reload.
  • No synonyms: "consent" finds "consented", not "agreement".
