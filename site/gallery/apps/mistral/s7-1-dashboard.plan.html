The plan this app was built from · Mistral Large 4

Goal

A dashboard for a diplomat, NGO or NHRI staff member to see at a glance what UN human rights mechanisms have said to Kenya: how much, when, on which themes, and to find the exact paragraphs quickly.

The data

File WORKSHOP_DATA.uhri; the country name is read from its country field, never typed in. Fields used: date, year, body, mechanism, type, symbol, doc_url, text, themes, sdgs, affected_persons, plus sdg_names for the SDG filter labels.

What the app shows

  1. Title "UN human rights recommendations to [country]", then one line: source, total number of records, earliest–latest year, all read from the file.
  2. A filter bar: dropdown for mechanism; dropdown for body (treaty body or mandate); two boxes for first and last year; dropdown for SDG (number and name); a keyword box; a "Reset" button. Any change updates everything below at once.
  3. A line "Showing X of Y records" plus a plain description of the active filters (e.g. "Treaty bodies · 2015–2020 · keyword: torture").
  4. A horizontal bar chart of the 10 most frequent themes among the matching records, longest bar on top, each labelled with its count. Note below: "A record with several items is counted once for each."
  5. A table of matching records, newest first: date, body, symbol (linked to the UN document, new tab), first line of the text. The recommending State is not shown as a separate column. Clicking a row expands it to show the full paragraph with its themes and SDGs; clicking again closes it. 100 rows at a time, with "Show more".

Rules for the code

  • Total and year range come from counting all records and taking the smallest and largest year.
  • A record matches when it passes every active filter; keyword search is case-insensitive on text. Only recommendations and concerns/observations are counted anywhere.
  • Chart: for each matching record add one per item in its list; sort by count, keep the top 10, break ties alphabetically.
  • Text stays verbatim: the first line is text up to its first line break or 160 characters plus "…"; the expanded view is the full text, unchanged.
  • Every row links symbol to doc_url. Missing doc_url: symbol shown unlinked. Missing symbol: "(no symbol)".
  • Missing date: "date unknown", sorted last. Missing body: "unknown". Empty themes or sdgs: "none recorded" in the expanded row; such records simply do not appear in the chart.
  • No matches: show "No records match these filters" instead of an empty chart.

How we will know it works

  1. On load, the title names your country and "Showing X of Y" has X equal to Y.
  2. Choose "Universal Periodic Review": the count, chart and table change together, and no row shows a treaty body like CEDAW or CRC.
  3. Type a rare word: the count drops and every visible row contains that word when expanded.
  4. Click a symbol: a docs.un.org page opens in a new tab with the same symbol.
  5. Click a row: the full paragraph appears word for word, including its paragraph number.

Not in this version

  • No comparison with other countries or with the global average.
  • No tracking of whether a recommendation was accepted, noted or implemented.
  • No AI summaries or translations; every word on screen comes from the file.
  • No export to Excel or PDF.
