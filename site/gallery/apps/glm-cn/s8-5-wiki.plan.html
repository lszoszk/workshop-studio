The request · GLM-5.3

Built on 4 November from an earlier plan with two more tabs, Ask and Ingest, which called a model; those tabs were taken out of the app on 11 November 2026. The request below is the current one.

Build a workspace for reading and checking our research wiki on neurotechnology in the administration of justice, one question per tab. Tab 01 Pages: the pages (sources, concepts, entities, questions) listed by type with a filter, and the index before a page is chosen; a page shows its type, register and status, its body rendered (headings, lists, [[links]] to other pages, contested blocks as cards with the verdict first), and under it Links out, Linked from and Sources. Show every pin where it stands in the sentence: the quote in quotation marks, then a chip with its label that opens the PDF at its page with `studio.openSource(id, page, quote)` (the studio marks the quote on the page), and a mark saying whether the code found the quote in that passage of the library (a quote with "…" is several fragments, each found in order). Tab 02 Check: one big number of pins verified by the code, and a lint table: dangling links, orphan pages, pins that fail, sources that differ from the documents pinned, contested cards missing from the tensions register, each with the pages concerned. Tab 03 Record: the log, newest first, the tensions register, and a download of the wiki. No model is called anywhere in this app: every number is counted by its code.
